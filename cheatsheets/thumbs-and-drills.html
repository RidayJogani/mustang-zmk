Thumb Cluster Map (Outer → Inner)

Left Thumb (LT1 → LT4)

LT1 (Outer) Hold → Layer 1 (Mouse & F1–F12)
LT2 (Middle) Tap: Enter · Hold → Layer 2 (Special)
LT3 (Index) Tap: Esc · Hold → LCtrl
LT4 (Inner) Tap/Hold: LAlt

Right Thumb (RT1 → RT4)

RT1 (Inner) Tap: Enter · Hold → LGui (Win/Cmd)
RT2 (Index) Tap: Space · Hold → LShift
RT3 (Middle) Tap: Space · Hold → Layer 3 (Arrows)
RT4 (Outer) Tap/Hold: Backspace
Design Highlight: You have two Enter keys (LT2 & RT1) and two Space keys (RT2 & RT3). While holding RT2 (Shift) with your right index thumb, tap RT3 for Space, or hit LT2 for Enter!

2-Minute Muscle-Memory Drills

Drill 1 — Symmetric Brackets & Braces

Hold LT2 on G H and B N; add RT2 (Shift) for curly braces.

Drill 2 — Coding Operators & Numbers

Stay on LT2: top row 1–0, home left & * # $, bottom left ! @ ^ ~, right - + _ = `.

Drill 3 — Navigation, Arrows & Mouse Scroll

Hold RT3 + H J K L for arrows; LT2 + outer pinkies for HOME/END & PG_UP/PG_DN; LT1 for Mouse (+ RT3 to Scroll).
